Privacy Policy

Where Are the Ducks?! (도대체 오리는 어딨어) · Studio Hankan
Effective date: November 5, 2026

Studio Hankan ("we") makes the puzzle game Where Are the Ducks?! This policy explains what information is handled when you play the game and how.

1. Information we collect

We do not directly collect any personal information, and we do not run a server for game data. You can play without creating an account or signing in.

Your progress, challenge records, item counts, purchase status, and settings are stored only on your device. They are deleted when you delete the game or its app data.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The iPhone and Android versions show ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, Advertising ID on Android) and other device information
  • Approximate location based on IP address
  • Ad interactions, and diagnostic data such as crashes and performance

On iPhone, the game asks for permission before tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", you still get ads, but they are not personalized. Rewarded ads (for hearts and items) only play when you tap the button to watch one. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Google handles this information under its own policy: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Purchases

The App Store and Google Play versions offer No Ads (lifetime or a monthly subscription) and a Hint Pack. Payments are processed entirely by Apple or Google. The game only checks whether a purchase was completed. We never receive your card or payment details.

4. Leaderboards

Leaderboards use Apple Game Center (iPhone) and Google Play Games (Android). Your scores and nickname are handled by those services under their own policies. You can play the game without signing in to them.

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information, and we do not outsource its processing. Google and Apple process information as described above under their own policies, and this may include processing outside your country.

6. Retention and your choices

  • We do not keep any personal information.
  • On iPhone, you can change tracking permission in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Deleting the app removes all game data stored on your device.

7. U.S. privacy rights (including California)

We do not sell or share personal information for money. Because we do not hold any personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. For data collected by Google for ads, you can use the choices in section 6 or Google's own tools.

8. Children

The game is rated for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Ads are limited to general-audience content.

9. Security

The game is distributed through the app stores, and our web pages are served over HTTPS.
