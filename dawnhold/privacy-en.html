Dawnhold Privacy Policy

Effective: 24 November 2026

STUDIO HANKAN ("we") sells the game Dawnhold ("the game") and follows applicable privacy laws. This policy explains what information the game handles.

Summary: The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no server, no ads and no in-app purchases. We do not collect, transmit or share personal information. Your game progress stays on your device.

1. Information we collect

We collect no personal information at all — no name, phone number, email address, address, location, contacts or photos. We run no server that receives game data, and the game works without an internet connection.

2. Information stored only on your device

The following is saved in the app's local storage on your device and is never sent to us or to third parties.

  • Progress: cleared stages, highest floor reached, star shards, collected guardians and summoners with upgrade levels, current deck
  • Settings: music, sound effects, haptics, damage numbers, volume, selected track

This data is deleted when you delete the app or clear its data. We cannot access it.

3. Ads and analytics

The game contains no advertising, and no ad, analytics or tracking SDKs are included. We do not request App Tracking Transparency permission and do not use any advertising identifier.

4. Purchases

The game is a one-time paid app with no in-app purchases. Purchase and payment information is handled by Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play) under their own policies; we never receive your card or payment details.

5. Third parties

We do not provide, sell or entrust your personal information to third parties. Distribution is handled by:

  • Apple Inc. — App Store · apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Google LLC — Google Play · policies.google.com/privacy

6. Children's privacy

Because the game collects no personal information and shows no ads, it collects no personal information from children or anyone else.

7. Your rights

We hold no personal information about you, so there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. You can remove device-stored progress by deleting the app.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here with a new effective date.
